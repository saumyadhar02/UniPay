INTRODUCTION TO THE PROJECT :
Secure PIN Authentication Digital payments have become a crucial part of daily life. Traditional banking apps often require internet banking, card details, or complex verification processes. Many users—especially students, small vendors, and rural citizens—need an easy, fast, and bank-free method to make transactions.
UniPay is a Bank-Free E-Wallet designed for simple and secure transactions using:
•	QR Code Payments
•	Pay-By-Number
•	Top-Up / Add Money
•	Transaction History
The system enables quick transactions without mandatory bank integration. Users can store wallet money, scan QR codes, and pay instantly. UniPay is developed to demonstrate how modern e-wallet systems work using simple, user-friendly, and secure components.

Problem Statement :
Many people face challenges such as:
•	Inability to use digital banking due to lack of bank accounts.
•	Delay in payments due to OTP or card verification.
•	No single, simple wallet for instant payments.
•	Lack of transaction history in small-scale payment tools.

UniPay solves these by providing :
•	A wallet that works without a traditional bank account.
•	Quick payments via QR code or mobile number.
•	A simple UI for all age groups.
•	Secure wallet PIN and transaction logs.
